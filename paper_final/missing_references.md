# Remaining reference work

The revised bibliography contains 31 entries. The claim-level audit in `reference_claim_matrix.csv` contains 33 claims: 10 are marked `SUPPORTED`, 6 `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, 16 `PRIMARY_SOURCE_NEEDED`, and 1 `UNSUPPORTED`.

The unsupported item is the explicit Clean Architecture attribution (`RC03`). The manuscript may describe separation of responsibilities as an implementation property, but it should not attribute that design to a named architectural doctrine unless an appropriate primary source is added and verified.

Items marked `PRIMARY_SOURCE_NEEDED` already identify recommended official documentation or original papers where available. Before submission, each cited record should be checked against the publisher or official project page, especially author order, title, year, venue, and persistent identifier. No DOI, volume, issue, page range, or ORCID should be added unless verified.

Project-specific quantitative claims do not require an external literature citation when they point to canonical repository evidence. They must instead cite the exact CSV, JSON, report, log, or campaign artifact recorded in `canonical_evidence_ledger.csv`.

The bibliography must not be expanded merely to increase its count. If a primary source cannot be verified, narrow or remove the associated claim.
